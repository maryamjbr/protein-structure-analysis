This repository documents a university course project in structural bioinformatics.

The project uses outputs and annotations from third-party scientific resources and software, including AlphaFold2, SWISS-MODEL, PyMOL, MolProbity, InterPro/InterProScan, DeepTMHMM, ExPASy, CATH, SCOP, MobiDB-related annotations, UniProt, and the Protein Data Bank (PDB).
